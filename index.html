About

SYZYGY Labs designs and builds exceptional technical exhibits for science museums, research organisations and innovative companies. We combine scientific expertise with creative design to create experiences that are engaging, memorable and technically rigorous.

From immersive digital experiences to precision-engineered physical installations, we work across disciplines to turn complex ideas into exhibits that inspire curiosity and spark conversation. We also provide expert consultancy, helping organisations develop ambitious projects from initial concept through to delivery.

Digital Experiences

We create interactive digital exhibits that make complex science accessible and engaging. Our work includes touchscreen applications, simulations, immersive visualisations, data-driven experiences and bespoke software designed for museums, visitor centres and corporate spaces.

Physical Installations

We design and build high-quality physical exhibits that combine engineering, craftsmanship and scientific storytelling. From kinetic demonstrations and interactive displays to one-of-a-kind installations, our exhibits are designed to be robust, elegant and unforgettable.

Consultancy

We provide specialist advice on scientific communication, exhibit design and technical implementation. Whether shaping the vision for a new gallery, developing interactive concepts, advising on emerging technologies or delivering workshops and professional training, we help organisations create experiences that are both innovative and impactful.
